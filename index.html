About Institution • छिन्चु इङ्लिस बोर्डिङ स्कुल

A Leading Academic Pillar of Karnali Province.

"Rooted in discipline, holistic growth, and English-medium excellence since 2042 B.S. (1985 A.D.)."

Located at Bheriganga Municipality-12, Chhinchu, Surkhet, Chhinchu English Boarding School (CEBS) has nurtured generations of doctors, engineers, legal professionals, managers, and educators across Karnali Province under the leadership of Principal Purushotam Timalsena.

Recognized by the Ministry of Education as the Best School in Karnali Province (2074 B.S.), CEBS offers an uninterrupted academic pathway from CEBS Montessori (Nursery–UKG) through SEE and +2 Science, Management, Law, and Education.

  • Nursery to Grade 12 Unified Campus
  • +2 Science, Management, Law & Education
  • Safe Residential Boarding / Hostel
  • MoE Karnali Best School Award (2074 B.S.)
